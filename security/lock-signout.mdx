---

These actions are intentionally different.

## Close the tab or browser

On the web, an unlocked vault can remain available on that device across refreshes and browser restarts. This is what makes Tuck practical for daily capture.

## Lock Tuck

Locking removes the locally stored unlocked vault key. Your encrypted data remains on the device and in sync, but Tuck needs the passphrase, Recovery Kit, or an allowed device-unlock method before it can read it again.

## Sign out

Signing out ends the account session and also removes the local unlocked vault key.

## iPhone and iPad

The native alpha app can use Face ID and Secure Enclave-backed protection to make repeat unlocks faster without storing the raw vault key in UserDefaults.

## Delete your account

In **Settings → Account**, choose **Delete account** and read the confirmation carefully. Tuck marks the account deleted immediately, stops renewal charges, and schedules active-system data for deletion after 30 days. Those 30 days are not a restore window.

For earlier erasure, contact [**privacy@tuckthis.app**](mailto:privacy@tuckthis.app). This action is different from locking the vault or signing out.
