Tuck uses plan entitlements to turn hosted features on or off while keeping the core capture and vault model consistent.

## Free

Core capture, vault, email/API ingest, todos, and opt-in Habits are part of the base experience.

## Pro

Adds SMS capture, calendar and mail connections, CardDAV contact sync, Pocket, and the option to use your own AI provider when enabled in the product.

Hosted MCP is available on Pro, Tucker, and Founder. In **Settings → Hosted MCP**, choose personal brains to publish as a separate readable copy for a compatible AI assistant. Full text is optional. Tokens start read-only; you can enable queued writes per token and choose writable brains. An unlocked device must seal those changes into the vault. See [Connect an AI assistant with hosted MCP](/use/hosted-mcp).

## Tucker and Founder

Include hosted Tuck AI and more storage. SMS uses a shared Tuck number; these plans do not promise a dedicated phone number.

## Storage and SMS allowances

| Plan | Storage allowance | SMS segments per month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 250 MB | Not included |
| Pro | 5 GB | 200 |
| Tucker | 100 GB | 200 |
| Founder | 200 GB | 200 |

Inbound SMS captures and outbound reminders share the allowance. Long messages and some characters use more than one segment. It resets each calendar month in UTC, with no rollover.

## Manage renewal

Open **Settings → Subscription** and choose **Cancel renewal** to stop the next renewal. Access continues through the paid period. If you change your mind before it ends, choose **Resume renewal**.

<Note>Prices and quotas can change as Tuck moves through early access. The in-product subscription screen is the source of truth for what you can purchase today.</Note>
