The native Tuck app for iPhone and iPad uses the same account, vault format, and capture grammar as the web app.

## Sign in and unlock

1. Sign in with a passkey or an email link.
2. If your account already has a vault, unlock it with the same vault passphrase.
3. After the first unlock, Face ID can make later unlocks faster according to your lock settings.

Do not create a second vault on your phone.

## Quick capture

Use Quick Tuck from the app, widgets, Shortcuts, or the Action button to get a thought into Tuck with as few taps as possible.

## Share into Tuck

Use the iOS share sheet to send text, links, and supported files from another app into Tuck.

<Note>The first mobile release is capture-first. Some web-only experiences, including calendar and mail connectors, the full rich editor, theme studio, and kanban, are intentionally not rebuilt on iPhone or iPad yet.</Note>
