The web app is the most complete place to use Tuck today.

Use it for:

- Fast capture with `⌘K` or `Ctrl+K`.
- Full rich-text editing.
- Brains and tags management.
- Stream, Columns, Calendar, and Kanban views.
- People records.
- Calendar and mail connections.
- Theme settings.
- Account, ingest, vault, and sharing settings.

## Locking the vault

While the browser is unlocked, Tuck keeps the vault key on that device so refreshes, closed tabs, and browser restarts do not require your passphrase every time.

Choose **Lock** when you want Tuck to remove that local unlocked key. Signing out also removes it.
