## If a send to a +1 number is rejected

If Telnyx rejects a send to a `+1` number and your account has an email address, Tuck emails the same message to that address. This includes verification codes and reminders.

Look for **Your Tuck verification code** for a code, or **A message from Tuck** for other fallback messages.

This fallback applies when the messaging provider rejects the send. It does not cover every text that fails to arrive after the provider accepts it, and it does not apply to numbers outside `+1`. The countries in the email-reminder table use email for reminders directly.

## Not available

Numbers outside the listed calling codes are not supported. This includes Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Japan, Australia, and India.

## Add your number

Add and verify your number in **Settings → Ingest**. Include the full country calling code. A ten-digit number without a leading `+` is treated as `+1`; a leading `+` is preserved.

If a supported number cannot receive its code, [contact support](/support/index). Never send support your verification code.

See [Capture by SMS](/capture/sms) for setup, allowances, and opt-out commands.
