Welcome to the Tuck documentation. Tuck is a local-first, end-to-end encrypted thought-capture app that gets out of your way: no folders to navigate, no friction between you and the page. Just tuck the thought, and your vault handles the rest — encrypting it on your device before anything leaves your hands.

<Note>
  Tuck is currently in early access. You need an **invite code** to create an account. If you don't have one yet, join the waitlist at [tuckthis.app](https://tuckthis.app).
</Note>

## Get Started in Four Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an invite and create your account">
    Enter your invite code at [tuckthis.app](https://tuckthis.app) and sign up with an email address and password. Your **account password** signs you into the app — it never touches your vault or your thoughts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your vault passphrase and save your recovery kit">
    Choose a strong vault passphrase — this is what unlocks your encrypted vault on each device you use. Then download your **recovery kit** and store it somewhere safe. Losing both your passphrase and your recovery kit means your thoughts are unrecoverable by anyone, including Tuck.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tuck your first thought">
    Open the composer from anywhere in the app and type whatever is on your mind. Hit **Tuck it** — your thought is encrypted on your device instantly. Add a tag or assign it to a Brain whenever you're ready, but you never have to before saving.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Capture from email or iPhone">
    Send a thought to your personal **send-to address** (`you@sendto.tuckthis.app`) from any email client to tuck it without opening the app. On iPhone and iPad, use the native Tuck app or the share sheet to capture anything from any app — unlocked with Face ID.
  </Step>
</Steps>
