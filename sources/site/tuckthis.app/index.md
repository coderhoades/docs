# Get it out of your head.

A thought shows up. You tuck it. You keep moving. Organization — tags, brains, a real editor — happens after the thought is safe. Text or email it in. Reminders come back even if this tab is closed.

- Capture first. Folders never gate the composer.
- Your vault encrypts the mind on this device. A passphrase unlocks it; a recovery kit saves you if you forget. [Why that is three secrets, not one](https://tuckthis.app/site/crypto).
- Signed-in devices sync ciphertext, not thoughts in the clear. [How the hosted pieces fit](https://tuckthis.app/site/architecture).
- You get a send-to address the moment you create an account. Until launch, that takes an invite code.
