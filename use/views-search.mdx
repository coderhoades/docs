Tuck does not force one way of looking at your notes.

## Stream

A chronological feed that works well for reviewing what you captured recently.

## Columns

A responsive card grid for scanning more thoughts at once.

## Calendar

Shows thoughts with dates. Use it when timing is part of the thought.

## Kanban

Uses brains as columns. Drag a thought to another column to move it to that brain.

Use **Add brain** to show another column. **Remove from kanban** hides a column without deleting the brain or its thoughts. Drag a column's handle to reorder it, or focus the handle and use the left and right arrow keys.

Column order and visibility are saved on this device. They do not sync to your other devices.

Moving a thought to another column keeps it visible on the board, including when you started from a single brain.

## Search and filters

Search across your vault and combine it with brain or tag filters to narrow the result.

## Pins and sorting

Pin important thoughts to keep them at the top of Stream or Columns. Those views can be sorted newest-first or oldest-first. Dated thoughts use their thought date, so imported Pocket recordings appear when the conversation happened rather than when you imported them.
