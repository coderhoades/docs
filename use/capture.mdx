The capture box is designed to be available before you have decided where a thought belongs.

## Open capture

Press `⌘K` on Mac or `Ctrl+K` on Windows and Linux to focus the capture box.

By default:

- **Enter** tucks the thought.
- **Shift+Enter** adds a new line.

You can swap that behavior in Settings.

## Add meaning while you type

- Type `#` for a tag.
- Type `@` for a person.
- Use `/brain` while capturing to file the thought into a brain.
- Add a date when the thought belongs on the calendar.
- Drop files into the composer or paste an image.
- Record audio when typing is the wrong tool.

## When Tuck thinks something is actionable

Tuck may show **This looks actionable** and offer to turn the capture into a todo, schedule it, or make it repeat.

Tuck checks the first line for specific action words, such as `call` or `send`, and phrases such as `need to`. See [Why a thought looks actionable](/reference/actionable-detection) for the complete rules, limits, and examples.

Prefix a capture with `*` to create a todo directly and skip the suggestion. This does not keep the capture as a thought only.

## Capture from Todos

On **Todos**, choose **Need to Tuck something away?** to open the thought composer. The keyboard shortcut works here too. Hiding the composer keeps your draft.

## Rename a thought from a list

On a card with a gray text preview, click the title to edit it in place. On a compact card, or a card without that preview, the title opens the thought. Edit its title there.

The brain chip on a thought card lets you open that brain or move the thought to another one.

For linked todos and repeat schedules, see [Use todos](/use/todos).
