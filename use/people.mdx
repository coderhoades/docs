People turn `@mentions` into reusable context.

A person can include a name, company, title, email addresses, phone numbers, websites, birthday, notes, a photo, and files.

Calendar invitees and mail addresses can help seed your People list.

## Mention someone

Type `@` in the capture box or editor and choose a person. The mention stays in the sentence.

## Merge duplicates

If the same person arrives through multiple sources, merge the records. Tuck shows a conflict picker when both records contain different values for the same field.

<Tip>Use People for durable context about someone. Use a brain for a project or area.</Tip>

## Import an address book

Open **People → Import vCard** and choose a `.vcf` file. Tuck matches contacts by email or phone and merges them with existing people. Importing the same file again skips contacts that are already saved. People you deleted stay deleted.

For an ongoing connection, use [CardDAV in Calendars & mail](/use/calendar-mail#connect-an-address-book).

## Choose whether mail adds people

In **Settings → Calendars & mail**, turn off **Add people from email** to stop From and To addresses on synced mail and imported `.eml` files from becoming People. This does not remove people already saved. Meeting invites can still add their invitees.
