Tuck can connect more than one calendar or mailbox.

Supported connection patterns in the current product include Apple, Google, Microsoft, Fastmail, Proton through ICS, CalDAV, and IMAP depending on the account type.

## Calendar context

When you capture during a meeting, Tuck can guess the meeting you are in and let you choose another event if the guess is wrong.

Invitees can seed your People list, which makes `@mentions` more useful later.

## Mail context

Pulled mail stays as context. Tuck does not automatically turn every email into a saved thought.

This keeps your vault from becoming a copy of your inbox.

<Info>Calendar and mail connections are available on paid plans according to your plan entitlements.</Info>

## Connect an address book

On the web app, open **Settings → Calendars & mail** and find **CardDAV**.

1. Choose **Apple**, **Google**, **Fastmail**, or **CardDAV** for another provider.
2. Check the **CardDAV URL** and enter your account email or username and **App password**. Use the credentials your provider supports for CardDAV.
3. Choose **Connect address book**.

This pulls contacts into **People**. Edits in Tuck are not written back to your address book. For Fastmail, use your sign-in email and an app password with Contacts access.

If the connection shows **Error**, choose **Fix issue**, correct the URL or credentials, and choose **Try again**. A failed connection stays visible so you can fix it.

CardDAV is available on Pro, Tucker, and Founder. You can also [import a vCard file](/use/people#import-an-address-book).

## Control people added from mail

The **Add people from email** switch controls whether From and To addresses become People. Turning it off keeps future mail addresses out of People, but meeting invites can still add invitees.

## Read your schedule

Choose a color for each calendar account in Settings. **Time Map** and the Briefing schedule use it to show where an event came from. Time Map includes a calendar legend and separates overlapping events into columns.

Titles with link styling open the associated thought, todo, meeting link, person, or brain when one is available. Briefing keeps its wider layout even when your thought feed uses Stream.
